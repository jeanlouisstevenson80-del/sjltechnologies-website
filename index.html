The company

One holding. Separate brands.

SJL Technologies LLC is a Wyoming-based limited liability company that wraps multiple products with clean liability isolation, one set of books, one tax relationship. Each product remains spinnable, licensable, or sellable separately when the time comes.

Design tokens, legal templates, infrastructure accounts and editorial voice are shared under the hood. Users never see "SJL Technologies" on the surface — they see Sprigful, Yarendo, CORVUS, JobConnect AI.

Operating principles

How we build.
What we refuse.

A single founder's biggest risk is scope creep. These are the rules the holding uses to resist it.

01 / FOCUS

One product at a time

Exactly one product gets the majority of build time. Others are maintained, incubating, or deliberately paused.

02 / BILINGUAL

EN + FR, parallel

Native French editorial review is a line item, not an afterthought. Translation is not localisation.

03 / PRIVACY

EU hosting by default

GDPR compliance treated as positioning. Health data gets explicit Article 9 consent, every time.

04 / HONESTY

No grift playbook

No "detox," no supplement sales, no influencer deals paid in product, no dark patterns. The refusal list is public.

05 / NARROW

Ship small, learn, expand

MVPs stay deliberately small — 25 recipes, not 25,000. Each product carries a defined kill-switch for AI costs.

06 / SPINNABLE

Each product stands alone

Separate repos, infra, and brands. If a product outgrows the holding, it can be spun out, licensed, or sold without untangling.

What SJL will not build

Explicit boundaries.

  • No crypto, Web3, NFT or token-based models
  • No gambling, betting or dating products
  • No AI that impersonates real people or fakes reviews
  • No weight-loss, calorie-tracking or "clean eating" products
  • No supplement, nutraceutical or health-product sales
  • No defence, surveillance or law-enforcement contracts
  • No agency or custom development work — product-only
  • No political campaign or activism contracts

Partner with the holding

Building something that fits our thesis?

We selectively partner on bilingual, privacy-first software — distribution deals, co-development, licensing. If your product refuses the same playbook, we want to hear from you.
