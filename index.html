The company

One holding. Separate brands.

Most software is built for users who already fit the dominant market: English-first, location-stable, and well-served by existing tools. SJL Technologies focuses on the opposite.

We build and hold digital products for people who work, learn, and live across borders — professionals navigating international opportunities, language learners who need practical results, and users who value clarity and privacy over noise.

Our role is not to operate every product day-to-day. Our role is to set direction, maintain focus, and support disciplined growth over time. Each brand stays spinnable, licensable, or sellable separately when the time comes.

Operating principles

How we build.
What we refuse.

A single founder's biggest risk is scope creep. These are the rules the holding uses to resist it — and stay worth trusting.

01 / START WITH THE REAL PROBLEM

Not trends

We evaluate every opportunity against strategic fit, user value, and long-term potential — not short-term trends. The problem must exist before the product does.

02 / BUILD WITH INTENTION

Disciplined, not reactive

We support disciplined product development, clear priorities, and measurable progress. MVPs stay deliberately small — scope expands only when beta data earns it.

03 / GROW RESPONSIBLY

Clear ownership, honest stages

We maintain clear ownership, accurate public information, and appropriate oversight across the portfolio. "Incubating" means incubating. The refusal list is public.

04 / RESPECT THE USER

Especially across borders

Especially those who work, learn, and live across languages and borders. GDPR compliance as positioning, not a chore. No dark patterns, no manufactured urgency.

What SJL will not build

Explicit boundaries.

  • No crypto, Web3, NFT or token-based models
  • No gambling, betting or dating products
  • No AI that impersonates real people or fakes reviews
  • No weight-loss, calorie-tracking or "clean eating" products
  • No supplement, nutraceutical or health-product sales
  • No defence, surveillance or law-enforcement contracts
  • No agency or custom development work — product-only
  • No political campaign or activism contracts

Partner with the holding

Building something that fits our thesis?

We selectively partner on bilingual, privacy-first software for cross-border and international users — distribution deals, co-development, licensing. A smaller number of focused products done properly beats a large portfolio of shallow experiments.
